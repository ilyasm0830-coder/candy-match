Delete your CandyMatch Adventure account

Option 1: inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open CandyMatch Adventure and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm (email accounts must enter their password; Google accounts confirm with Google).

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the game, email ilyasm0830@gmail.com from the address used for the account (or tell us your username). Subject: “Delete my CandyMatch account”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, username, avatar and profile photo, all saved progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, scores, badges) and your leaderboard entry. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

See also our Privacy Policy.

🏆Collect badges and finish special challenges

How to play

1
Swipe a candy next to another one to swap them.
2
Match 3 or more of the same candy to pop them. Match 4 for a rocket and 5 for a rainbow bomb.
3
Reach the target score before your moves run out to win up to 3 stars.

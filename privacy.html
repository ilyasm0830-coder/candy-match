Privacy Policy

CandyMatch Adventure is developed by Muhammad Ilyas. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account we store: your username, your email address (email sign-up) or your Google account identifier and name (Google sign-in), your chosen avatar and optional profile photo, and your game progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, scores, badges and settings). Guest players are given an anonymous identifier and a generated name such as “Guest1234”. We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address or location.

How it is used

The data is used only to run the game: to sign you in, save your progress so you can continue on any device, and show the public leaderboard. We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and the game currently shows no ads.

Leaderboard

Registered (non-guest) players appear on a public leaderboard that shows username, avatar, frame, total stars and level. Your profile photo is never shown to other players. Please never use your real name as a username.

Where it is stored and security

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access rules allow each player to read and change only their own saved data. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and we cannot see them. Firebase may process technical data such as IP address to keep the service secure.

Children

Children under 13 should only play together with a parent, using the parent’s email address or as a guest. Parents can ask us to delete a child’s data at any time.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time inside the game (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by following the steps on our Delete Account page. When an account is deleted we delete its save data, profile photo and leaderboard entry. Guest accounts that are never used again may be removed after a period of inactivity.

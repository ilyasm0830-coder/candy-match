Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you create a profile we store your nickname, your chosen avatar and your game progress (levels, stars, coins, badges and settings). Your nickname, avatar, total stars and highest level are shown on a public leaderboard that anyone who opens the game can see, so please never use your real name. We do not ask for real names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or location. Your PIN is used only to sign you in and is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

How it is used

The data is used only to save your progress so you can continue on any device. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase may process technical data such as IP address to keep the service secure. Playing as a guest keeps data only on your own device.

Children

The game is designed to be child-friendly. We collect as little data as possible. Parents can ask us to delete a child’s profile at any time.
